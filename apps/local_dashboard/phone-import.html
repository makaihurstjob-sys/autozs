Import a product

Save a Home Depot product as an eBay draft. Your desktop worker captures the price, photos and details while you browse on your phone.

Keep the AUTOZS computer and Chrome worker online. Imports wait for capture and your review; this action does not schedule or publish them. Your iPhone must be connected to your private Tailscale network.

Set up Share → Import to AUTOZS
  1. Open Apple Shortcuts and create a shortcut named Import to AUTOZS.
  2. In the shortcut’s Details, enable Show in Share Sheet and set the accepted input to URLs.
  3. Add URL Encode, choose Encode, and use Shortcut Input as its input.
  4. Add Text. Paste the prefix below, then append the URL Encoded Text variable directly after source=.
  5. Add Open URLs, using that Text as its input.

In Safari, open a Home Depot product, tap Share → Import to AUTOZS, then tap Save draft on the page that opens.
